# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community experience.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful and constructive
- welcome different viewpoints
- focus on technical and product clarity

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, hate speech, or abuse
- sharing private or proprietary material without permission
- disruptive or malicious conduct

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or users that violate this policy.

## Reporting

Please open an Issue and mark it as conduct-related.
